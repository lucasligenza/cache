Terms of use

Draft dated 10 October 2026. These are not a contract until they are published with a real contact address.

The service

CacheNotes (“cache”) lets you capture notes, file them in directories, review them, and set reminders. The website is hosted on Vercel. Accounts, notes, and reminders are stored in Supabase. See the privacy policy for what is stored and who handles it.

Your notes

You keep ownership of the notes you write. You give us permission to store them, show them back to you, and — if you turn reminders on — send a short excerpt through your browser’s push service.

You are responsible for what you store. Don’t use the app to break the law or to harm other people.

Accounts

An email account can reset its password. A guest session is an anonymous account: logging out of it in this browser ends that session, and the notes that never synced anywhere else go with it. Creating an account from a guest session keeps the notes.

You can delete your account yourself, from Settings. You must type delete. That removes your notes, directories, and push subscriptions on the server, then deletes the login. Export first if you want a copy. We can’t restore a deleted account.

Availability

The app is provided as-is. Notes can fail to sync, reminders can fail to send, and the service can be interrupted. If a note matters, export it. We are not liable for lost notes, missed reminders, or anything that follows from using the app.

We may suspend an account that abuses the service, including one that tries to store other people’s data or overwhelm the service.

Changes

This draft will change before it is in effect. The published date and the contact address will be updated when it is.
